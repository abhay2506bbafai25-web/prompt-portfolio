Overview

This repository contains an AI Prompt Portfolio created for a Business AI/ML case study. It includes structured prompts from four business perspectives:

Finance
Human Resources (HR)
Marketing
Sales

Each portfolio contains 25 prompts, progressing from broad and basic questions to focused, advanced, and strategic AI/ML applications.

Business Problems Covered
Business Perspective	Illustrative AI/ML Problem
Finance	AI-based Loan Default Prediction
HR	AI-based Employee Attrition Prediction
Marketing	AI-based Personalized Marketing & Customer Segmentation
Sales	AI-based Lead Scoring & Sales Forecasting
Prompt Structure

The 25 prompts in each portfolio are divided into five tiers:

Tier 1 – Broad Exploration

Introduces Artificial Intelligence and its general applications within the business domain.

Tier 2 – Industry / Domain Focus

Narrower prompts that focus on specific business functions and use role-based or comparative questioning.

Tier 3 – Specific Business Problem

Focuses on one concrete business problem and explores required data, ML approaches, workflows, and expected business benefits.

Tier 4 – Solution Design & Critical Evaluation

Moves toward practical AI/ML solution design, including architecture, risks, ethics, governance, real examples, and evaluation.

Tier 5 – Strategic & Expert-Level Synthesis

Uses executive-level prompts involving implementation roadmaps, self-verification, stress testing, and final business-case development.

Prompting Techniques Used

The portfolio demonstrates progressive use of:

Objective

The objective of this portfolio is to demonstrate how prompts can be designed progressively—from simple AI-related questions to advanced prompts that support business analysis, AI/ML solution design, critical evaluation, and strategic decision-making.

Note

The business problems and company references used in the portfolios are illustrative and can be adapted to a specific organization or industry.
